One tiny rule.
Infinitely detailed pictures.

We iterate zn+1 = zn2 + c, starting from z0 = 0. Depending on c, the result either stays close to zero forever or races off to infinity.

Colour every c that stays close and you get the Mandelbrot set.

A possible path through the lesson

  1. ① z + w: addition is just "walking" one arrow, then the other.
  2. ① z · w: put w on the unit circle and drag it: multiplying is turning. Press i · i = ? to see why i² = −1.
  3. ① z² and zⁿ: inside the unit circle, powers shrink to 0. Outside, they explode.
  4. ②: now add c after every squaring. Try c = 0, −1, i, 1 from the presets and step through by hand.
  5. Turn on Square, then add c to link back to part ①.
  6. Drag c slowly across the edge of the shadow. Inside it the orbit stays bounded, and just outside it escapes.
  7. Tick Shadow uses only N steps and slide N up from 1: the set takes shape step by step.
  8. Click 🔭 Explorer to view the Mandelbrot set on its own and zoom around it.
